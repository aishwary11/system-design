    Method, formulas & sources

    Monthly capacity uses a 30-day month (2,592,000 seconds) and 730 hours. Storage is steady-state retained bytes; first-month growth is not modeled.

    • Traffic: avg RPS = requests/month ÷ 2,592,000; peak RPS = avg × peak factor.
    • Concurrency: in flight = peak RPS × average duration (Little’s law).
    • Compute: instances = max(minimum, ceil(peak RPS ÷ (RPS/instance × target utilization) × redundancy)).
    • Storage: retained GB = daily GB × retention days × replicas ÷ compression.
    • Egress: TB/month × 1,000 × $/GB.
    • AI: tokens = requests × tokens/request; cache savings apply only to cacheable traffic that hits.
    • LLM serving: GPUs = ceil(monthly tokens ÷ tokens per GPU-month).
    • Observability: samples/day = series × 86,400 ÷ scrape seconds; logs are divided by compression; traces by sample rate and retention.

    The ~70% utilization warning comes from the queue-cliff discussion in Concepts §52. Preset sources: AI gateway, observability, LLM inference, webhook delivery.

    Model totals can differ from a document’s rounded table because this page derives compute and egress from their inputs. The webhook preset prices egress at a commodity ~$0.09/GB, while the webhook document’s $3/GB line is a conservative all-in reference estimate (retries, cross-AZ/region transfer, NAT and premium egress). Edit the egress price to compare the two; the source tables remain the reference.
